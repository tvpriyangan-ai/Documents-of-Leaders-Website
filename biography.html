Biography

Demis Hassabis is a British artificial intelligence researcher, neuroscientist, and entrepreneur, widely recognized as the co-founder and CEO of Google DeepMind.

Under his leadership, DeepMind has produced landmark breakthroughs such as AlphaGo and AlphaFold, reshaping how the world understands the potential of artificial intelligence. In 2024, his contributions to science were honored with the Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

Born on July 27, 1976, in London.
He is 50 years old as of 2026.
